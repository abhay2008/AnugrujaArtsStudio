A bit about Anuradha

Anuradha Govarthanan is a certified art teacher, accomplished artist, and curator with over 20 years of experience. She holds diplomas in Graphic Designing, Commercial Arts, and Fine Arts, along with specialized certifications in Tanjore, Mysore, Kerala Mural, Resin, and Lippan Arts. Renowned for her expertise in various painting mediums such as watercolor, acrylic, oil, and pastels, Anuradha has curated numerous exhibitions and conducted workshops for multinational corporations and foreign exchange programs.

Her work has garnered widespread recognition, earning her prestigious awards from organizations like the Department of Language & Culture and government-recognized NGOs. Anuradha's passion for nurturing artistic talent has impacted thousands of students globally, guiding them in mastering diverse art forms and securing admissions to top design and architecture universities.

In addition to teaching, Anuradha accepts commissions for customized paintings, including portraits and traditional art, and sells her artwork. Her profound contributions continue to inspire and enrich the global art community.

...

Anuradha began her artistic journey under the guidance of Shri Jayaraman and Shri Maruthu, learning the nuances of line drawings and realistic art from Shri Jayaraman and mastering water mediums and landscapes from Shri Maruthu. She further enhanced her skills in traditional Indian art under Shri Ganesan and Shri Ramesh. Anuradha's deep passion for traditional Indian art forms is evident in her specialization and mastery of techniques like Tanjore, Mysore, and Kerala murals.

In 2012, Anuradha founded Anugruja Arts, offering comprehensive coaching for art entrance exams, intermediate and higher grade exams, and short-term and diploma courses in fine arts. Her teaching philosophy focuses on cultivating creativity, technique, and a deep appreciation for diverse art forms, helping students achieve their artistic aspirations and build strong portfolios for admissions to leading institutions in India and abroad.
