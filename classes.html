Online & Offline Classes

Classes throughout the year

• Basic drawing and painting classes for kids (offline, small batch of 6 students only). • Advanced classes (watercolor, acrylic, oil painting, pencil sketching, charcoal) on weekends for kids and adults. • One-on-one classes on weekdays for all age groups (Tanjore, Kerala mural, Mysore traditional paintings). • Coaching for entrance exams: NATA, NID, NIFT, CEED, UCEED, B.F.A. • Govt-recognised Higher grade, Intermediate grade, Elementary exam coaching.

Short Term Courses

2 to 3 months intensive training

• Medium-based masterclasses: Charcoal, Soft Pastels, Oil Pastels, Watercolor, Acrylic, Oil Painting. • Traditional Art forms: Tanjore, Mysore, Kerala Mural, Lippan, Madhubani, Kalamkari, Mandala, Resin Art. • Specialized Courses: Portrait Drawing, Human Anatomy, Perspective Drawing, Composition.

Diploma Courses

1 Year Comprehensive Fine Arts Program

• Complete foundation in fine arts covering all major painting and sketching mediums. • Art history, aesthetics, color theory, live model sketching, and outdoor landscape study. • Solo exhibition preparation and professional portfolio building.

Summer Camps & Special Workshops

Creative workshops during vacations

• Annual Summer Art Camp featuring mixed-media explorations, pottery, fluid art, and craft workshops. • Corporate team-building art workshops and weekend intensive creative sessions.
